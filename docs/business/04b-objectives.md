# clew · Business Objectives

This document defines the outcomes clew v1 MVP must achieve. Objectives are qualitative, inspiring, and time-bounded; Key Results are measurable state changes (never feature deliveries). The discipline follows Doerr's OKR framework, BABOK v3 §1.3, Adzic's outcome-over-output rule, and Kaplan & Norton's Balanced Scorecard perspectives.

Methodology and bibliography: see the [`business-objective` skill in homemade-claude-kit](https://github.com/VictorHueni/homemade-claude-kit/tree/main/business-objective).

**Scope:** clew v1 MVP. The OKR set expires when v1 ships.

**Companion docs:**
- [`docs/VISION.md`](../VISION.md): the north star these objectives operationalise.
- [`docs/business/01a-personas.md`](01a-personas.md): the personas whose outcomes these objectives serve.

**Upstream gaps to backfill:** BMC value propositions (`VP-NN`) remain undefined; the VPC drill-down is deferred until external interviews surface gaps wave 1 did not catch. Capabilities are defined in [`03a-capability-map.md`](03a-capability-map.md); each OBJ below carries a `Realised by:` line mapping its KRs to L1 capabilities. Value streams are defined in [`04a-value-streams.md`](04a-value-streams.md); VS-1 (Compose), VS-2 (Navigate), and VS-3 (Refactor) are fully filled and wired into the OBJ blocks below. VS-4 (Share) is also fully stage-decomposed, but at Medium overall pain and Assumed confidence (no consumer instance yet; Tier-2 / Backlog priority) — per [04a OI-0013](04a-value-streams.md#open-items), VS-4 ratings must not drive engineering priority over VS-1/2/3 until a second user reads the published snapshot.

## Confidence Legend

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Assumed | Hypothesis from author judgement. No external evidence yet. |
| Tested | Probed with at least one structured interview, prototype, or measurement. |
| Validated | Confirmed across multiple independent sources or in real-world use. |

### OBJ-03 · Validate the core hypotheses before scaling

- **Perspective:** Learning
- **Timeframe:** clew v1 MVP
- **Owner:** Victor Hueni (founder)
- **Why it matters:** Every artefact in the project today (VISION's audience claim, P-01's frustrations and goals) is assumption-based. Locking in a substrate without validating the underlying hypotheses risks building a tool for a persona that does not exist outside the author's head.
- **Linked from:** [Lean Canvas UVP](02a-lean-canvas.md#3-unique-value-proposition--confidence-tested-n1-founder-as-instance-with-refinement) · [P-01 §Research Grounding](01a-personas.md#research-grounding) (partially researched, wave 1 complete 2026-05-24, next review 2026-08-22) · [Wave-1 synthesis](../discovery/interviews/research-synthesis-2026-05-24-P-01-validation.md)
- **Realised by:** OBJ-03 is a research / validation objective; its KRs (persona conversion, interview programme, dogfood adoption, frustration labelling) are activities, not capabilities. No `Realised by:` mapping; capabilities serve OBJ-01 and OBJ-02 outcomes that OBJ-03 then validates.
- **Confidence-cliff watchpoint (KR-03.2).** Every pain rating in [`04a-value-streams.md`](04a-value-streams.md) currently rests on N=1 (founder-as-instance). These ratings are *descriptive* today (documenting Ava's lived experience). They become *prescriptive* the moment a rating is cited to prioritise one engineering item over another, e.g., "build C4.1 before C3.3 because VS-3.2 is Critical." Wave-2 (KR-03.2) must happen **before** that descriptive-to-prescriptive crossover, otherwise N=1 evidence is silently driving a roadmap. **Trigger to run wave-2:** when a planning conversation cites a pain rating as the deciding factor for sequencing engineering work, pause and run wave-2 before committing the priority. The target of "≥ 1 non-product role" matters here: wave-2's primary job at the crossover is to test whether the pain ranking generalises beyond the founder, not to confirm magnitude.

**Make-or-break assumption to test at wave-2 (persona ↔ rigor).** The single largest strategic risk ([competitive scan 2026-07-07](../discovery/competitive-landscape-2026-07-07-agentic-architecture-tools.md), [ADR-0013](../architecture/decisions/adr-0013-minimal-model-not-repo-native-ea.md)) is not whether the pain is real but whether P-01 — a solo / 1–3-person agent-first builder — will actually *author the upstream rigor* (capability map, value streams, objectives) rather than only the spec/plan layer. The methodology depth is enterprise-architect-grade; the persona is a solo agent-hacker. Wave-2 must probe: *when you start a project, how far up the strategy stack do you actually model — and would you fill in a five-node capability graph, or only requirements/specs?* If the answer is "only specs," the mandatory model shrinks toward the spec→plan spine and the upstream artefacts become optional enrichments, not the core. Do not deepen the upstream metamodel before this is answered.
